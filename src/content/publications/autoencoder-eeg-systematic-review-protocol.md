abstract: |
  This registration sets out the protocol for a systematic review and evidence map
  of peer-reviewed empirical studies using autoencoder-based models with recorded
  non-invasive scalp EEG. The review will examine architectures, EEG representations,
  application areas, latent-space designs, interpretation methods, validation
  practices, methodological quality, and reproducibility. Searches will cover Scopus,
  Web of Science Core Collection, PubMed, and IEEE Xplore. Springer Nature Link will
  serve as a supplementary publisher-platform search. Backward and forward citation
  searching will supplement the electronic searches. The final reproducible searches,
  formal screening, extraction, methodological appraisal, and synthesis had not
  started when this registration was submitted.

Registered protocol for a systematic review and evidence map of peer-reviewed
empirical studies using autoencoder-based models with recorded non-invasive scalp
EEG, registered on OSF Registries with Morteza Akbari and Luca Longo. The protocol
fixes the review questions, search strategy (Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection,
PubMed, IEEE Xplore, plus backward and forward citation searching), eligibility
criteria, and the extraction and appraisal plan before screening begins, so the
review itself is reproducible end to end. Saheed independently screens a
reproducible random 20% sample, with the random seed and selected study IDs stored
before screening.
